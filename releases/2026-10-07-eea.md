## In this image

- Titan Controls and the hardware keyboard / trackpad stack
- USB HID
- Atlas Debian plane (`atlas_linux`, about 4.1 GiB)
- OpenWrt plane (`atlas_openwrt`, 128 MiB)
- IMS, microG, OpenEUICC, stock FM, USB audio, Nanobot
- Cube icon mask
- 1:1 camera crop path (Aperture UHD crop)
- Tablet UI, density `cube300`
- No GApps
- No Moonlight

`boot.img` in the download is the stock kernel, sha256 `2c307d8958c16c145e284efb5bb967c497827b6c6951f42319c1ef038c2d47df`. The rest of the stock boot images (`vendor_boot`, `init_boot`, `dtbo`, `vbmeta*`) are from that same firmware train. `init_boot.img` is stock, not Magisk or KernelSU.

## Not in this download

Commits on `main` after 2026-10-07 14:45 +0300 are not in this super. That includes the later keyboard-map unification, the Plasma account guard, the IME-bar fixes, and the 1:1 video crop. Those are source changes only until the next image.
